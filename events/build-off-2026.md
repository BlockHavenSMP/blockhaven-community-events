# The Great Overworld Build-Off — 2026

**Theme:** Forgotten Civilizations  
**Coordinator:** Casey Brooks (`CraftingCasey`)

Create a Minecraft build telling the story of a lost civilization. A ruined city, an abandoned temple, a buried settlement, or another original interpretation all fit.

## Rules
1. Use the designated community event area.
2. One finished build per person or team.
3. Include a short description of the civilization.
4. Submit by **October 31, 2026**.
5. Winners will be announced **November 2, 2026**.

Three judges were originally planned. EnderArtist has withdrawn due to a scheduling conflict. Casey is organizing replacements; Alex must confirm the final lineup.
